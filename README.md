# Ayla & Kameel's Super Obstacle Course

A web platformer starring **Ayla** (Super Jumper!) and **Kameel** (Super Speedy!).
It runs in the browser with no installs and no build step.

## How to play

| | 1 player | 2 players (same keyboard) |
|---|---|---|
| Move | ← → or A D | Ayla: A D · Kameel: ← → |
| Jump (hold for higher) | Space, ↑, W or Z | Ayla: W · Kameel: ↑ |
| Swap kid | X, C or Shift | – |

- **P** pauses and **M** mutes.
- On phones and tablets, on-screen buttons appear. It works in landscape and portrait, and in 2-player mode each player gets their own set of buttons.
- In 1-player mode you can swap between Ayla (jumps higher) and Kameel (runs faster) at any time.
- Stomp the Grumbles, hopping Boings, sliding penguins, birds and cannonballs. Never stomp the red spiky ones!
- Stomp several enemies without touching the ground for a combo. Five in a row earns an extra life.
- Hit **?** blocks for smiley coins and power-ups:
  - 🪽 **Wings** give you a double jump.
  - ⭐ **Super Star** makes you invincible for a while.
  - ❤️ **Heart** gives you an extra heart.
- Find the **3 big stars** hidden in every world.
- Collect 50 smiley coins to earn an extra life. Touch a green checkpoint flag to save your spot.

## Worlds

1. **Sunny Meadow**: springs, moving platforms and falling platforms
2. **Penguin Peaks**: slippery ice, sliding penguins and falling icicles
3. **Smiley Skies**: a sky-high obstacle course across clouds
4. **Lava Castle**: fire bars, crushers, cannons, lava and the boss, **King Grumble**
